# Applied AI Engineer CV Design

## Objective

Create a modern, credible master CV for Kareem Ahmed that primarily targets junior Applied AI Engineer and software-engineering internships while remaining persuasive for freelance clients.

## Positioning

- Primary title: Applied AI Engineer.
- Present Kareem as an independent builder of production-oriented software and AI-enabled workflows, not as an employee of the projects or brands.
- Support the AI title with implemented model-backed product workflows and agent/automation design; distinguish these from conventional full-stack systems.
- Emphasize shipped systems, technical ownership, bilingual Arabic/English product development, privacy, security, testing, and deployment verification.
- Do not include age, date of birth, photograph, phone number, WhatsApp number, home address, or unsupported metrics.

## Audience

1. Junior-role and internship recruiters.
2. Engineering reviewers and hiring managers.
3. Freelance clients seeking end-to-end product and automation work.

## Deliverable

- A polished, ATS-compatible two-page master CV in DOCX and PDF.
- Page one must work as a self-contained recruiter-ready overview.
- Page two provides additional project and technical depth.
- Single-column layout with conventional headings, selectable text, restrained typography, and no skill bars, icons, decorative graphics, or content-bearing headers/footers.

## Content Structure

1. Kareem Ahmed — Applied AI Engineer.
2. Email, GitHub, and portfolio links only.
3. Concise professional summary.
4. Core technical skills grouped by capability.
5. Selected engineering projects, led by the strongest evidence of applied AI and multi-surface product ownership.
6. Additional projects.
7. Minimal education entry: CNISE, Cambridge curriculum.
8. Languages and relevant strengths.

## Evidence Rules

- Derive project statements from repository code, documentation, configuration, tests, and commit history.
- Do not claim user counts, revenue, conversion improvements, employment, client engagements, or production status without repository or live evidence.
- Use concise accomplishment bullets that communicate problem, engineering action, and product value without fabricated numerical outcomes.

## Visual System

- Formal, contemporary, ATS-safe business-document styling.
- US Letter portrait, compact but readable margins, strong black hierarchy, one restrained blue accent, and consistent spacing.
- No tables for main CV content; links remain clickable.
- Use typography and whitespace—not graphics—to create hierarchy.

## Verification

- Inspect source evidence for every substantive project claim.
- Audit the DOCX for ATS-readable structure and consistent style tokens.
- Render the DOCX to page images and inspect every page at full size.
- Produce a PDF from the visually approved DOCX and confirm page count and text extraction.
